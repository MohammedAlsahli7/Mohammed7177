Tab'a (طبعة, "a print") — a squiggle of purge filament with a floating face. The cheerleader of the crew: hypes the customer, panics about deadlines. Pair with <Goblin> (the judge).

```jsx
<Mascot pose="cheer" color="yolk" swirl="pla" size={160} track />
```

pose: hello · printing (strands draw in — loading) · cheer (success, hyping the user) · oops (failed print) · sleepy (idle/empty) · shout (yelling across the shop). color/swirl = two real filaments. track = eyes follow cursor.
